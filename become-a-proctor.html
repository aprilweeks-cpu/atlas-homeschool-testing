Become an ATLAS Test Administrator

The Arkansas Department of Education is seeking qualified educators to assist with remote administration of ATLAS assessments for participating homeschool students.

Complete Interest Form

Who Can Apply?

  • Retired & Current Teachers
  • Retired & Current Administrators
  • Former & Current School Test Coordinators
  • Former & Current District Test Coordinators
  • Other Experienced Educators

Responsibilities

  • Attend required training
  • Administer remote testing sessions
  • Follow test security requirements
  • Communicate with assigned families
  • Report testing irregularities as needed
